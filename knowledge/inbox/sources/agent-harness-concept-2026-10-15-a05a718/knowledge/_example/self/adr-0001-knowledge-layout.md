# ADR 0001: Preserve Discovered Knowledge Categories (Illustrative)

This is a worked proposal, not an actual project decision or human acceptance.
The cited files describe the policy that motivates the illustration.

## Context

An adopting project may already have suitable knowledge paths and protections.
Copying an example tree can create duplicate categories and broken retrieval.

## Decision

Propose reuse of discovered suitable paths, with approval before adding or moving
categories. An adapter would configure the actual startup entry and check layout.

## Reasons

Existing navigation and evidence relationships are easier to preserve when the
adapter follows the target's conventions.

## Rejected alternatives

Unconditional copying of the source example would impose unverified paths and
could violate category protections.

## Consequences

Discovery and explicit classification require work before writes. Future curation
would retain focused pages and maintain affected navigation and checks.

## Open questions

The actual target paths, protections, and required check command depend on discovery.

## History

This illustrative record is Proposed. It has no retained human decision evidence
and asserts no actual acceptance or status transition.

## Supersession

No replacement is asserted by this illustration.
